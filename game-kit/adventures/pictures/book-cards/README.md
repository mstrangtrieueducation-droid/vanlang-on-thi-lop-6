# Phonics picture cards

389 local illustrations extracted from the Student Cards in the four Oxford Phonics World books supplied by the centre. The books and their URLs are recorded in `khoa-hoc/phonics-units.cjs`; `manifest.json` records each card's book and printed page (PDF page is one higher). The full PDFs are not bundled with the game.

Book pages were rendered with the long edge at 2,000 pixels, cropped to the individual illustration (excluding the printed word label), rotated upright, and saved as lossless PNG. Contact sheets were checked against the word lists before publication. These are textbook illustrations, not newly generated artwork. Copyright remains with the respective rights holders.

The blank image alt text is intentional inside quiz buttons: spoken prompts and numbered button labels carry the accessible instructions without exposing the answer. In word-to-picture matching, the button announces the pupil's current pairing.
